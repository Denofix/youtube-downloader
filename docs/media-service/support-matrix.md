Pinterest individual pins enter native image/GIF/video delivery; Instagram photo/carousel posts enter the bounded selection workflow. Ongoing streams and collection URLs are rejected; TikTok photo-post URLs enter the bounded photo workflow. Share links are resolved by the worker with a five-hop limit, exact host/path validation and same-platform redirects. See stage-2.md for extractor review, verified scope and remaining live gate.

Presets expose actual behavior: Original uses exact source streams; Audio MP3 has an explicit bitrate; Smaller Video encodes H.264 CRF 25 or VP9 CRF 34 with no guaranteed size reduction; GIF Clip has a maximum 15 seconds, 24 FPS and 720-pixel width. Increasing bitrate or resolution does not restore detail.
